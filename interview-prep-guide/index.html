1. Behavioral / HR Stories
2-3 days

Every company starts here. Your project portfolio is exceptional — you just need 8–10 crisp STAR stories pre-built so you're not inventing them live.

  • Write stories for: SMS Cross-Sell ($2.3M), PSM Study (76% lift), Propensity Model ($500K), AskData Bot, JustAnswer retention (+11%)
  • Prepare for: biggest failure/lesson, disagreement with stakeholder, ambiguous project, end-to-end ownership, influencing without authority
  • Prep 'Why this company' answers for each target — research their recent product launches first
2. Product Sense + Metrics Frameworks
3-4 days

All 7 companies test product sense. Your instincts are strong from SMS work, but you need a repeatable framework you can run in real time — not just project recall.

  • Master the metric drop framework: segment → external vs internal → leading indicators → funnel step → A/B test hypothesis
  • Practice 'define success metrics for X' for messaging platforms, CRM tools, marketing automation, subscription SaaS
  • Drill: North Star vs supporting vs guardrail metrics — why each one exists
  • Practice out loud: 'SMS engagement dropped 15% — what do you do?' using your actual SMS funnel knowledge
3. Experimentation / Stats
2 days

Bayesian A/B, PSM, AIPW, DiD — you've done all of it. The gap is explaining these cleanly to a non-technical interviewer.

  • Prep a 90-second explanation of Bayesian vs frequentist A/B testing (why you prefer Bayesian for early stopping)
  • Know: p-value vs posterior probability, Type I/II errors, power, MDE — explain each in plain English
  • Practice the 'metric is up — was it causal?' question using your PSM study as the answer
  • Be ready for: 'How would you design an A/B test for feature X?' — use your SMS cross-sell experiment as a template
4. ML / DS Fundamentals
3-4 days

Google, Microsoft, ServiceNow, and Klaviyo will probe ML depth. Your XGBoost/LightGBM work is strong, but expect questions on model internals, regularization, and class imbalance.

  • Review: bias-variance tradeoff, regularization (L1 vs L2), tree-based model internals (XGBoost vs LightGBM vs RF)
  • Class imbalance: SMOTE, class weights, downsampling — you used downsampling in the propensity model, know the tradeoffs
  • Know your model choices: 'Why XGBoost vs logistic regression for propensity?' — have a crisp answer
  • SHAP: you used it in the churn model — explain SHAP values vs feature importances and why SHAP is better
  • Review: cross-validation, train/test leakage, and why your SHA512-NTILE split was the right call
5. SQL Live Assessment
1 day

You're already advanced. The only gap is interview-speed SQL — writing window functions under pressure without IDE autocomplete.

  • Practice 5–10 medium problems on StrataScratch (product-analytics style, not algorithmic)
  • Refresh: LAG/LEAD, DENSE_RANK, self-joins for retention cohorts, funnel step analysis
  • Practice writing a DAU/WAU/MAU query and a 30-day retention cohort query from memory

Story Bank — 6 Pre-Built STAR Stories

SMS Cross-Sell Experiment $2.3M revenue · 96% Bayesian confidence

Headline: $2.3M revenue impact — 96% Bayesian confidence

Use for: Tell me about your biggest project, business impact, A/B testing experience, working with stakeholders

Key Numbers: $2.3M attributable revenue, 96% Bayesian posterior probability, 3 treatment arms, 60K users

Depth Signal: The Bayesian framing was a deliberate choice — Bayesian allows early stopping without inflating Type I error, which frequentist testing can't do cleanly in email experiments with variable open rates.

PSM Study — Integrations Causal Inference 76% retention lift · AIPW doubly-robust

Headline: 76% retention lift with Canva integration — causal proof

Use for: Causal inference questions, observational study design, influence without a controlled experiment

Key Numbers: 76% retention lift, AIPW doubly-robust estimator, Canva integration, 6-month post-period

Depth Signal: AIPW over PSM because AIPW is consistent if either the PS model or outcome model is correctly specified — doubly robust. PSM alone gave wrong-sign estimates when the PS model was misspecified early on.

SMS Propensity Model $500K revenue · XGBoost + SHAP

Headline: $500K in targeted upsell revenue — propensity-to-purchase model

Use for: ML modeling questions, feature engineering, production model deployment, business impact

Key Numbers: $500K, XGBoost + SHAP, 180+ features, 76% precision at top decile

Depth Signal: The class imbalance challenge: 3% positive rate. Used downsampling + class weights + threshold tuning. SHAP let us explain predictions to the SMS product team so they could sanity-check the top-scored accounts.

AskData Bot (Semantic Layer) Gemini 2.5 Pro · Vertex AI · Semantic Layer

Headline: End-to-end AI analytics assistant — Gemini 2.5 Pro on Vertex AI

Use for: AI/ML engineering questions, autonomous agent design, cross-functional influence, innovation

Key Numbers: Gemini 2.5 Pro, Vertex AI, 50+ metrics in semantic layer, 10+ teams using it

Depth Signal: The hard part wasn't the LLM — it was grounding it in a trusted semantic layer so it couldn't hallucinate metric definitions. Built a YAML-based knowledge layer that the model cites.

JustAnswer Retention Analysis +11% retention · Subscription redesign

Headline: +11% retention from subscription model redesign

Use for: Product analytics questions, defining metrics for subscription SaaS, early career story

Key Numbers: +11% retention, subscription model, cohort analysis, 3-month intervention timeline

Depth Signal: Identified that the drop-off was happening at day 14, not day 30 as assumed. This changed the intervention strategy from email re-engagement at day 25 to product friction removal at day 12.

SMS Churn Propensity Model LightGBM · Temporal split · 90-day prediction

Headline: Predicting which SMS subscribers will churn before they disengage

Use for: End-to-end ML pipeline, label definition challenges, temporal validation, production deployment

Key Numbers: 5-stage Dataform pipeline, LightGBM, temporal train/test split, 90-day prediction horizon

Depth Signal: The label definition was the hardest part: SMS 'churn' isn't account closure — it's disengagement. Defined churn as no campaign send for 90 days after a period of active sending. Temporal split was critical to avoid leakage.

Behavioral / HR Q&A

Preparation Rule

Write out your answers to all of these before your first screen. Do not improvise. Each spoken answer should be 2–3 minutes max.

"Tell me about yourself"

Frame: Mailchimp DS journey (SMS + ML focus) → AskData Bot (AI innovation) → now targeting Senior DS/PA at companies doing AI + analytics at scale. One sentence on why this company specifically. End with: 'I'm excited to bring this to [Company] because...'

"Tell me about your biggest failure"

Tell the propensity model story but frame the failure as an earlier version that ignored class imbalance and had terrible precision. Learned: always look at the full precision-recall curve, not just AUC. Applied the lesson to the final model.

"Tell me about a time you disagreed with a stakeholder"

SMS experiment framing: PM wanted to declare success at 2 weeks based on email opens. You pushed back — needed 4 weeks to see purchase conversion. Built the Bayesian monitoring framework to show daily posterior probability, which let you stop early WITH statistical confidence when the signal was clear.

"Describe a time you worked on an ambiguous problem"

AskData Bot: no clear spec, no existing system, no precedent at the company. Approach: (1) defined the metric taxonomy first (what is a 'metric'?), (2) built a prototype with 5 metrics, (3) used it in a real stakeholder meeting, (4) iterated based on what broke.
